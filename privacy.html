Your information

How Flawed Hero uses your information

In plain words. Last updated 6 November 2026.

Flawed Hero is the app for The Flawed Hero Marathon and the events around it. It is run by Phil Roberton, founder of Flawed Hero, and built and looked after by Michael Broadbent and the It Takes a Village collective. Phil and Michael are the "organisers" below.

What we hold about you

  • Your account: your email address and a password. We never see your password.
  • Your profile: your name, photo, mobile number, address, emergency contact and the "why I'm a Flawed Hero" story, but only what you choose to fill in.
  • What you add as you use the app: your movement log, your charities, your medals and the photos of medals you send in, your marathon booking tick, events you plan to attend, messages and suggestions you post, and your notification settings for your phone.

Your Strength Benchmark results stay on your own phone. We do not receive or store them.

Who can see it

  • Only you and the organisers: your email, mobile number, address and emergency contact.
  • Other members: your name and photo, your Flawed Hero Marathon medals and the medals you upload, and anything you post on the Message Board once it is approved. We will check with you before your "why I'm a Flawed Hero" story is shown to anyone else.
  • Everyone, without names: combined totals, such as how far all the heroes have moved together.

Why we use it

  • To run the events, approve members and keep you safe (that is what the emergency contact is for).
  • To show your medals, charities and movement in your own cabinet and log.
  • To send you notifications about things that matter to you, such as an approval or a reply. You can switch these off in Settings.

Where it is kept

  • In a database run by Supabase, in London. Photos and medal pictures are stored there too, with long, unguessable addresses. They are not listed anywhere, but anyone who had the exact address could open one.
  • The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Finished medal pictures are published with it.
  • Notifications travel through your phone maker's service (Apple, Google or your browser's).
  • The app loads fonts from Google Fonts and a small code library from jsDelivr, so those services can see your internet address when you open it.
  • Shop payments are taken by Stripe, outside this app. Zoom sessions run on Zoom.
  • The app keeps you signed in and remembers your settings using your phone's own storage. There are no adverts and no tracking.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. If you delete your account, your profile, log, medals, bookings and photos are deleted. Anything you posted on the Message Board stays, but with your name removed.

Your choices

  • See or download your data: Settings, then "Download my data".
  • Change it: edit your details any time in My Profile.
  • Delete it: Settings, then "Delete my account".
  • Ask us anything: speak to Phil or Michael through Useful Contacts.
  • If you are not happy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
